## Photographic Language Principles & Hard Prohibitions (§17)

### Hard Fails (§17.4):
- No clenched fists or exaggerated victory celebrations as default visual language
- No trophy lifting or celebration imagery as the main representation of performance
- No athletes screaming into the camera
- No generic gym photographs with no connection to the page subject
- No people staring intensely into the distance
- No decorative brain graphics, lightning effects or mental-toughness clichés
- No therapy clinic / hospital stock imagery
- No scientists in laboratory coats used to create an impression of science
- No abstract gradients as the only visual vocabulary of the website
- **Nothing implying a laboratory, facility, staff team, programme or technical capability that MindGame Africa does not have**

### Sourcing Rules (§17.5 & Prompt 8):
- **GENERATE**: Shot type is `wide environmental` or `quiet analytical` — abstract, architectural, or graphic data visualization motifs that do not center on identifiable people performing specific jobs. Status: `PLACEHOLDER`.
- **STOCK**: Shot type is `portrait`, `group`, `medium working`, `close detail`, or `movement` — depicting specific people in authentic roles. Sourced by humans from licensed libraries. Status: `MISSING`.
- **SUPPLIED**: Depicts an actual identifiable real person tied to the brand (e.g. Founder Dr. Joshua A. Oparachukwu). Never generated or stock-substituted. Status: `MISSING` (cross-references Outstanding Asset #3).
