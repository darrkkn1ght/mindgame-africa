# MindGame Africa — Stock Imagery Download & Procurement Checklist

This checklist contains only the **STOCK** image slots required by `docs/image-manifest.md`. It provides a dedicated hand-off for a human researcher or procurement team to batch-download licensed photography from stock libraries (such as Getty Images, Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, or specialized sports archives) and drop them directly into the codebase without re-deriving file paths or dimensions.

---

## Hard Prohibitions — What to Avoid (§17.4)

Before licensing any image, ensure it **does not violate** the client's hard rules:
- ❌ **No clenched fists** or exaggerated victory screams/celebrations
- ❌ **No trophy lifting** or medals
- ❌ **No athletes screaming into the camera**
- ❌ **No generic commercial gym shots** (dumbbells, generic fitness models)
- ❌ **No people staring intensely into the distance**
- ❌ **No decorative brain graphics, lightning bolts, or "mental toughness" clichés**
- ❌ **No therapy clinic or hospital stock imagery**
- ❌ **No laboratory coats** used for scientific effect
- ❌ **No abstract gradients** as the primary subject
- ❌ **NOTHING implying a laboratory, facility, staff team, or capability MindGame Africa does not have**

All images must feature **African athletes, practitioners, coaches, researchers, and environments** (§17.2).

4. Save the file with the exact **Target File Path** (e.g. `public/images/teaching-and-learning/home-education-seminar-4x3.jpg`).
5. In `docs/image-manifest.md`, update the status from `MISSING` to `SUPPLIED`.
